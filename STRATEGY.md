# Arcora Strategy

## Target problem

People starting a new role have no plan that fits their actual role, level, team shape, or company context — so they improvise their ramp. The first 90 days are too high-stakes and compressed for trial-and-error: probation, first impressions, and manager confidence all get locked in before anyone has time to figure things out by guessing.

## Our approach

Win by being the lightweight daily ritual that turns a 90-day plan into compounding small actions — beating generic checklists on cadence and adaptability rather than depth on day 1. The bet: ramp succeeds when small role-fit actions stack daily, not when a perfect plan is delivered week one.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** IC knowledge worker starting a new role at a new company (PM, designer, engineer, marketer, ops). They're hiring Arcora to ramp confidently and align with their manager early — without figuring out what "good" looks like by guessing.

## Key metrics

- **Activation: signup → first plan generated** — % of signups that complete onboarding intake and generate a usable first plan. Front-door health.
- **Daily-rhythm retention (D7 / D30 / D60 active)** — % of activated users who return on N+1 days through the 90-day window. Direct test of whether the 3-minute ritual compounds.
- **Phase progression (30 → 60 → 90 completion)** — % of users advancing through phase milestones in real time. Tests whether the plan tracks lived experience instead of being abandoned.

## Tracks

### Plan generation quality

The AI + role intelligence that produces a role/level/team-aware plan on day 0.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The daily ritual only compounds if each day's tasks are actually right for this person — quality of the seed determines value of the rhythm.

### Daily ritual UX

The today/tasks/notifications surface that makes the 3-minute morning rhythm stick — cadence, friction, mobile, push, adaptive replanning.

_Why it serves the approach:_ This is the approach in surface form — if the daily ritual isn't frictionless and present, no compounding happens.

### Knowledge & enrichment

RAG / kb / company research / templates — feeds each daily task with role-specific and company-specific context so it's not generic.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Generic daily actions stop compounding fast; enrichment is what keeps the next 89 days worth opening.

## Marketing

**One-liner:** Your first 90 days, three minutes at a time.

**Key message:** A role-aware 90-day plan that turns into a daily ritual — small actions that compound into a confident ramp.
